PRIVACY POLICY

Last updated: October 26, 2026

This policy explains how VOID JUMP handles information when you use the iOS game.

Information stored on your device

VOID JUMP stores your selected character locally on your device. This preference is used to remember your character selection.

Advertising

VOID JUMP uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. Google and its partners may process information such as advertising identifiers, device information, and ad interactions to deliver, measure, and protect ads, subject to applicable settings and consent requirements.

Learn more from Google's Privacy Policy.

Game Center

If you use leaderboards, Apple Game Center handles player authentication and leaderboard features. Your Game Center player information and submitted scores may be processed by Apple under Apple's privacy terms.

See Apple's Privacy Policy.

Sharing and retention

Information processed by advertising providers and Apple Game Center is subject to those providers' practices. Locally stored preferences generally remain on your device until cleared or the app is removed.

Children and privacy choices

Privacy choices for advertising may be available through your device settings or any consent options presented in the app, where applicable. Parents and guardians should review device privacy settings for younger users.
