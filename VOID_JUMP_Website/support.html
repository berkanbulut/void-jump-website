Player support

HOW CAN WE HELP?

Need help with VOID JUMP? Find quick answers below or contact the developer.

How do I play?

Tap to control your character, avoid obstacles, and try to beat your highest score.

How do I change my character?

Open Characters from the main menu and select your preferred character.

How do leaderboards work?

VOID JUMP uses Apple Game Center for leaderboards. Make sure Game Center is enabled on your device, then tap Leaderboard in the game.

How do I report a problem?

Please contact ahmed.berkan.bulut.no@gmail.com. Include your device model, iOS version, and a brief description of the issue. Do not include passwords or sensitive information.
